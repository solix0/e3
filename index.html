How to Use
1. Share Camera (Host): Click "Share camera", grant camera permissions, and share the generated 4-digit PIN or QR code with the viewing device.
2. Connect to Camera (Viewer): Click "Connect to camera", enter the 4-digit PIN or scan the host's QR code to view the live stream.
3. Connection Issues: If direct connection fails due to network restrictions, try enabling Relay mode on both devices.
